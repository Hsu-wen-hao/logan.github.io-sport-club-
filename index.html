About Velocity

Championing athletes across every discipline

Velocity Sports Club delivers a complete athletic ecosystem. Our mission is to cultivate community, competitive spirit, and high-performance pathways for athletes ranging from youth rookies to seasoned pros.

  • Comprehensive youth, collegiate, and adult programs.
  • Nutrition, physiotherapy, and recovery labs on site.
  • Community-first culture with over 15,000 members.
